LFV — Sweden
Luftfartsverket · Sweden
Live · Since 2015
World's first ICAO-certified operational remote tower. LFV with Saab's r-TWR system controls Örnsköldsvik from Sundsvall since April 2015. Extended to multiple Swedish airports from Stockholm Arlanda.

Avinor — Norway
Avinor · Norway
Live · 15+ Airports
Avinor operates remote tower services across 15+ small Norwegian airports from centralised RTCs. World leader in large-scale deployment; uses Saab and Frequentis systems. Home to one of the world's largest RTCs.
Saab r-TWR ↗ Frequentis ↗ 15+ Airports ↗ RTC Bodø ↗ Indra ↗
↗ Avinor Digital Towers

NATS — United Kingdom
NATS · United Kingdom
Live · London City 2022
NATS Digital Tower at London City Airport — first digital tower at a major international hub. Controllers use 4K panoramic cameras and AI-enhanced video. Also operational at Southampton.
Frequentis ↗ Searidge ↗ London City Airport ↗ ▶ 4K Video + AI ↗ Southampton ↗

DFS — Germany
DFS Deutsche Flugsicherung · Germany
Live · Leipzig RTC
DFS operates the Remote Tower Centre (RTC) in Leipzig, controlling Erfurt-Weimar airport remotely. One of Europe's most advanced operational RTCs, using Frequentis system with panoramic cameras.
Frequentis ↗ Leipzig RTC ↗ Erfurt-Weimar ↗

IAA — Ireland
Irish Aviation Authority · Ireland
Operational
IAA pioneered Multiple Remote Tower Operations (MRTO) research and testing. Focused on managing multiple Irish airports from a single RTC. Leading human factors research partner in SESAR.
MRTO Research ↗ SESAR ↗ Saab · Multiple Airports ↗

HungaroControl — Hungary
HungaroControl · Hungary
Operational · 2022
HungaroControl operates a remote tower from Budapest, controlling Győr-Pér airport. First operational remote tower in Central-Eastern Europe; one of the world's most advanced setups according to Indra.

Austro Control · Austria

Austro Control exploring remote tower implementation for smaller Austrian aerodromes. Collaboration with Frequentis (headquartered in Vienna) on system development and operational trials.
